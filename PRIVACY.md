# Privacy Policy — Dachuan X2MD

_Last updated: 2026-11-08_

Dachuan X2MD does **not** collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data.

- **Local processing.** All conversion happens inside your browser. Page content is never sent to the developer or to any third-party server.
- **Storage.** Your options, optional AI prompt and recent conversion history are saved with Chrome's `storage` API on your own device. Options may sync through your Google account if Chrome Sync is enabled. You can clear the history from the converter page at any time.
- **Network requests.** The extension makes its own requests only when the converter page's "Public API" fallback runs. In that case it asks `api.fxtwitter.com` and `cdn.syndication.twimg.com` for the public data of the post link **you entered**. No identifiers are sent.
- **Send to AI.** When you click "Send to Claude / ChatGPT / Gemini", the Markdown is copied to your clipboard and the chosen site opens in a new tab. What you do there is governed by that service's own privacy policy.
- **Permissions.** `x.com` / `twitter.com` access is used only to read the page you're converting. `contextMenus` provides the right-click menu. `storage` saves your settings. `activeTab` lets the popup read the current tab.

Questions: please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
